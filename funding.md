# FUNDING Policy

## Purpose
This document describes the intended mechanism for redistributing revenue generated by Space Leaf Corp’s Digital Sea project. The policy is a governance template to ensure transparency and alignment with the project mission: support nonprofit children’s STEM and safety education, sustain program partnerships, and fund continued R&D.

## Revenue Sources
Revenue may include licensing, sponsorships, consulting, demonstrations, ceremonial events, donations, and grants.

## Allocation Model
By default, incoming net revenue will be allocated as follows:
- **60% to Nonprofit Fund for Kids** — scholarships, educational kits, community workshops, and safety training for children and youth.  
- **25% to Program Funding and Partnerships** — sustaining partnerships and program commitments. Payments to corporate partners require a signed partnership agreement.  
- **10% to Project Reserve** — operational costs, infrastructure, and R&D reinvestment.  
- **5% to Administrative and Compliance Costs** — legal, accounting, and audit expenses.

## Stewardship Board Oversight
- A Stewardship Board oversees fund distribution. Changes to allocation percentages require a 2/3 vote of the Board.  
- The Board consists of at least three independent members: one technical lead, one nonprofit representative, and one independent auditor or legal advisor.

## Partnership Payments
- Any payment to external corporations (for example Microsoft or Tesla) requires a written partnership agreement signed by both parties.  
- Corporate contributions are intended to support joint programs and must be documented in governance records.

## Transparency and Reporting
- Quarterly financial reports will be published in **/governance/financials/**.  
- Annual independent audit reports will be posted and summarized in the repository.

## Granting and Disbursement Process
- Grant proposals for nonprofit programs are submitted to the Stewardship Board.  
- Emergency disbursements follow a documented fast track approval process.

## Donations and Sponsorships
- Donations intended for the nonprofit fund should be routed through the project’s registered nonprofit entity.  
- Corporate sponsorship proposals must include scope of work and draft partnership agreement.

## Amendments
- This policy may be amended by a 2/3 vote of the Stewardship Board. All amendments must be published in **/funding/**.

## Legal Notice
This policy is a governance template and does not constitute legal or tax advice. Consult qualified legal and tax professionals before accepting or disbursing funds, registering a nonprofit, or entering partnership agreements.
