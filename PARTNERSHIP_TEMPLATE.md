# Partnership Agreement Template

**Parties**
- **Space Leaf Corp**  
- **Partner Organization** (e.g., Microsoft or Tesla)

**Purpose**
Define scope, financial commitments, deliverables, reporting, and governance for joint programs related to the Digital Sea project.

**Scope of Work**
- Description of joint program, deliverables, timeline, and responsibilities.

**Financial Terms**
- Total contribution amount and payment schedule.  
- Allocation of funds and permitted uses.  
- Requirement that corporate payments be used only for agreed joint programs.

**Reporting and Audit**
- Partner receives quarterly program reports.  
- Annual independent audit of funds related to the partnership.

**Intellectual Property**
- Specify IP ownership, licensing, and permitted use of project marks and seals.

**Liability and Indemnification**
- Standard clauses allocating risk and indemnities.

**Termination**
- Conditions for termination and disposition of remaining funds.

**Signatures**
- Authorized signatories for Space Leaf Corp and Partner Organization with dates.

**Legal Notice**
This template is a starting point. Consult legal counsel to finalize a binding agreement.
