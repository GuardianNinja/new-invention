# Stewardship Board Charter

## Purpose
The Stewardship Board ensures transparent, ethical, and mission-aligned distribution of funds and oversight of the Digital Sea project’s governance.

## Composition
- **Technical Lead** — responsible for technical review and safety oversight.  
- **Nonprofit Representative** — represents beneficiary programs for children.  
- **Independent Auditor or Legal Advisor** — ensures compliance and financial integrity.  
- Additional seats may be added by unanimous Board vote.

## Responsibilities
- Approve annual budget and allocation percentages.  
- Review and approve partnership agreements and corporate contributions.  
- Oversee quarterly financial reporting and annual audits.  
- Approve emergency disbursements per documented protocol.  
- Maintain public records of decisions in **/governance/board_minutes/**.

## Decision Making
- Routine decisions by simple majority.  
- Allocation changes and policy amendments require a 2/3 vote.

## Meetings
- Quarterly meetings minimum. Emergency meetings may be convened as needed.

## Conflict of Interest
Board members must disclose conflicts and recuse themselves from related votes.

## Amendment
This charter may be amended by a 2/3 vote of the Stewardship Board.
